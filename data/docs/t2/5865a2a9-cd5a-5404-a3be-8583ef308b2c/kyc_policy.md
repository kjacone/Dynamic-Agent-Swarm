# KYC / AML Policy (Sample)

> Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not legal advice. Review with your compliance team before production.

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
All new customers undergo CDD before the account is opened. CDD means:
- Identify the customer and verify identity from a primary document.
- Understand the nature of the customer's business and expected activity.
- Screen against sanctions and politically-exposed-persons (PEP) lists.
- Assess money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk.

## Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
EDD applies when:
- The customer is a PEP or is associated with a PEP.
- The customer is a resident or national of a high-risk jurisdiction.
- The risk score is at or above the EDD threshold (default 70/100).
- Sanctions or adverse media screening returns a positive match.

EDD requires two compliance officers (four-eyes rule) to approve before the account opens.

## Sanctions and PEP
Screen every customer before onboarding and on an ongoing basis. A positive match is not automatically a decline —
it is a manual-review trigger.

## Records
Retain every decision, the documents reviewed and the approver trail for the statutory retention period.

## Refusal to onboard
If identity cannot be verified, or if the customer refuses to provide requested information, do not open
the account. Record the refusal and reason.
