# NMB Marketplace help (sample)

This is synthetic demo content, not the bank's live policy.

## Delivery

- Orders inside Dar es Salaam usually arrive in 1-3 business days; up-country
  deliveries take 3-7 business days.
- Sellers must dispatch paid orders within 48 hours.
- Delivery fees are shown at checkout.

## Payments

- Mobile money, debit and credit cards, and cash on delivery where the seller
  supports it.
- Payment is held until the order is delivered.

## Returns and refunds

- Unused items in original packaging may be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Perishables, digital goods and personalised items are not returnable.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days of the
  returned item being received.

## Selling on the marketplace

- Complete merchant onboarding with a business licence and TIN, then create a
  store.
- Listings are reviewed before they go live.
- Sellers see order notifications and payouts in the merchant portal.

## Data protection

- Buyer and seller data is processed under the Kenya Data Protection Act.
- Phone numbers and identity documents are used only for verification and
  fulfilment, and are never shared with other buyers.
- Full card numbers are never stored by the marketplace.
