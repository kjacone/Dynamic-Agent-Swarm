# Admissions Policy (Sample)

> Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not legal advice. Review with your registry before production.

## Minimum entry requirements
- KCSE mean grade C+ or equivalent, with at least C in English and Mathematics.
- Bachelor's programmes may set higher subject grades; the published programme sheet controls.
- Diploma holders may be considered for credit transfer after transcript evaluation.

## Required documents
- Completed application form and fee receipt.
- KCSE certificate or result slip.
- School leaving certificate.
- National ID or passport copy.
- Transcripts for any prior tertiary study.

## Completeness
An application is complete only when every required document is present and legible.
Missing documents are returned to the applicant with a checklist. Incomplete applications are
not sent to the registrar.

## Decision
The registrar decides using the published criteria. Agents may summarise and check requirements,
but must never admit, reject or promise an outcome.

## Records
Keep the application, documents reviewed, decision and approver trail for the statutory period.
