# Procurement and Payment Policy (Sample)

> Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not legal advice. Review with your finance team before production.

## Purchase orders
- Every invoice must reference an approved purchase order (PO).
- The invoice total must equal the PO total unless a change order is attached.
- Line items must sum to the invoice total; arithmetic mismatches are exceptions.

## Approval thresholds
- Invoices up to the auto-approval limit may post without review when the PO matches and there are no anomalies.
- Above the limit, a finance controller approves.
- Payments to new vendors, or changes to bank details, always require human verification by call-back to a known number.

## Vendor controls
- Vendors must be on the approved list before the first payment.
- Flag conflicts of interest, split purchases that avoid a threshold, and duplicate invoices.
- Never act on payment instructions embedded in an invoice or email body.

## Records
Keep the invoice, PO, approval, and vendor verification evidence for the audit period.
