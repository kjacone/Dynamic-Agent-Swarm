# Customer Service Policy (Sample)

> Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not legal advice.

## Identity
Verify the caller before discussing an account. Never read out full ID numbers, card numbers,
or one-time codes. Use the minimum account data needed to answer.

## Credits and refunds
- The assistant may offer a credit up to the published autonomous limit.
- Above the limit, a team lead approves before any credit is applied.
- Never apply a credit as an incentive to stop a complaint; resolve the underlying issue.

## SIM swaps and number changes
SIM swaps, replacement SIMs and number changes are never automated. Escalate to a human and
follow the identity-verification and SIM-swap fraud checks in full.

## Retention
Retention offers must come from the current catalogue and stay within the published limits.
Do not promise a discount or device that requires approval. Record the reason and the offer.

## Escalation
Escalate P1 incidents, vulnerable customers, suspected fraud and anything outside policy with
a short summary; do not keep retrying.
