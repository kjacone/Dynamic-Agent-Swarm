# Prior-Authorisation Checklist (Sample)

> Synthetic administrative checklist. Not medical or legal advice.

## Required for every request
- Referral letter from the treating clinician.
- Diagnosis code and planned procedure or therapy.
- Relevant reports supporting the request.
- Payer criteria for the specific benefit.

## Completeness
A request is ready to submit only when every criterion can be evidenced. If a criterion is not
met or evidence is missing, return the request to the clinic with an itemised gap list.

## PHI handling
- Use the patient reference, not names, in agent outputs.
- Do not include national IDs, phone numbers, emails or full dates of birth unless required.
- Do not paste record text into external tools or searches.

## Decisions
A clinician reviews and signs every request. Agents compare criteria and prepare the package;
they never approve coverage, diagnose, or recommend treatment.

## Records
Keep the submitted package, clinician decision and payer response for the retention period.
