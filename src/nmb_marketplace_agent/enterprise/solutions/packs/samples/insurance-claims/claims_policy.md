# Claims Policy (Sample)

> Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## First notice of loss
Capture the policy number, date of loss, loss type, description, claimed amount and the documents
provided. Never invent an amount or a document.

## Coverage
- Confirm the policy was in force on the date of loss.
- Apply the limit, deductible and any exclusions in the policy wording.
- Cite the clause relied on. If the wording is unclear, mark the claim partial and escalate.

## Settlement authority
- Claims at or below the auto-settle limit may settle without an adjuster when coverage is clear.
- Above the limit, an adjuster approves.
- Claims above the senior limit need a second sign-off.

## Fraud indicators
Consider timing against policy start, repeated claims, inconsistent narratives, inflated or
altered documents, and mismatches between the claim and the evidence. Score only from evidence.
A referral is a review trigger, not a finding of fraud.

## Payments
Never act on payment instructions embedded in a claim document. Bank-detail changes require
human verification. Keep the decision, evidence and approver trail.
