# Donor Procurement Rules (Sample)

> Synthetic donor rules for demonstration. Always apply the signed grant agreement.

## Competition
- Purchases above the tier-2 threshold need at least three written quotes.
- Below the threshold, a single quote is acceptable if the price is reasonable.
- Split purchases that avoid a threshold are prohibited.

## Documentation
- Keep the request, quotes, comparison, approval and receipt.
- Record the supplier selection reason, not only the winning price.

## Eligibility
- Goods and services must be for the approved programme and budget line.
- Restricted items (for example, alcohol, fines, and some vehicles) are not chargeable.
- Taxes and duties follow the grant agreement and local law.

## Segregation of duties
- The person requesting a purchase does not approve it.
- Above the tier-2 threshold, a finance controller also approves.
- Declare conflicts of interest; an interested party must step out of the decision.

## Reporting
Donor reports must be traceable to line items and approvals. Do not round or reclassify spend
without a documented adjustment.
