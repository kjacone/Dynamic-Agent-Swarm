# Service Charter (Sample)

> Synthetic service information for demonstration. Not legal advice. Publish your own charter.

## Small business trade licence
Required with every application:
- Business registration certificate.
- KRA PIN certificate.
- Premises lease or ownership document.
- Fire-safety certificate for the premises.
- Application fee receipt.

## Service standards
- Completeness check within 1 working day.
- Officer decision within the published SLA (default 72 hours).
- Applicant informed of the decision and the appeal route in writing.

## Incomplete applications
Return the application with an itemised checklist. Do not forward it to an officer until every
required document is present. The applicant keeps their place in the queue once complete.

## Decisions and appeals
An authorised officer decides. Refusals must state the reason and how to appeal within the
published appeal window. Agents may draft notices but must not grant or refuse a permit.

## Records
Keep the application, documents, decision and officer identity for the statutory retention period.
